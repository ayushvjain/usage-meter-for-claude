## Description

Usage Meter for Claude is a small desktop widget that shows how much of your Claude plan you've used, right on your desktop.

It sits on the desktop like a large icon: behind your app windows, on your icon grid, and in view whenever you look at the desktop. No need to open claude.ai settings to check where you stand.

It works with Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise accounts, and with accounts that belong to more than one organization. It shows whatever limits claude.ai reports for your account, so new limits appear automatically.

Free and open source. Your sign-in stays on your computer, and the app only talks to claude.ai: no analytics, no third-party servers.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Claude is a trademark of Anthropic.

## Product features

- Current session (5-hour) usage with a reset countdown
- Weekly limits, including per-model and per-product limits, with reset times
- Peak hours, shown in your local time
- Lives on the desktop, behind your apps, snapped to your icon grid
- Sizes itself to your usage; drag the corner to make it bigger
- Cozy and Classic styles, light and dark themes, accent colours and opacity
- Refreshes every 5 minutes, or whenever you click refresh
- Works with several organizations on one account
- Free and open source; your data stays on your computer
