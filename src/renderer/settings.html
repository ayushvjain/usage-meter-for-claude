Settings

General

How often the widget checks your usage. Refreshing by hand restarts the countdown.

Weekdays 13:00 to 19:00 UTC, shown in your local time.

Open the widget when you sign in to Windows.

Appearance

Theme

System follows Windows' light or dark mode.

Accent colour

Used for the usage bars and buttons. Bars still turn amber and red as you near a limit.

Lower values let more of your wallpaper show through.

Widget size

The widget snaps to your desktop icon grid like a large icon: 3, 4 or 5 icon tiles wide. Drag it by its title.

Position

Move the widget back to the top-left corner of your main screen.

Custom CSS

For developers: restyle anything with your own CSS file. Changes apply when you save it.

Account

Your account belongs to more than one. The widget shows the one you pick.

claude.ai

Open claude.ai in the widget's own browser window, for example to pass a browser check.

Your sign-in stays on this computer, in the widget's own browser profile. The widget only talks to claude.ai. Signing out deletes it.

About

Usage Meter for Claude

A free, open-source desktop widget that shows your Claude session and weekly usage, reset times, and peak hours.
